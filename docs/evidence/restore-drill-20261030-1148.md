## Finding: RPO target not met

The measured RPO (369 s) exceeded the 5-minute target by 69 s. No data was lost, because
the last write (11:24:55) preceded the restore point (11:30:10), but the potential
data-loss window was 6 min 9 s. RDS ships transaction logs to backup storage about
every five minutes, plus upload delay, so a 5-minute RPO cannot be guaranteed by
point-in-time recovery alone.

Actions:
- The RPO objective for point-in-time restore is revised to **<= 10 minutes**, in line with
  how RDS recovery actually works (`scripts/restore-drill.sh` now checks this target).
- The RTO objective (<= 30 min) is unchanged; measured 12 min.
- A tighter RPO needs a different mechanism, not a different target: Multi-AZ
  (synchronous standby, near-zero RPO for an instance or AZ failure) or Aurora. This is
  documented as a production recommendation and is not enabled in the sandbox for cost.
